### Trust-Hash Protocol
**A Decentralized Data Provenance and Thermodynamic Distillation Protocol**
*"Because you wouldn't consume food without knowing its ingredients and origin, AI models and critical systems should not consume data without verifiable physical provenance."*

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### 1. Overview & Problem Statement
As synthetic data generated by large language models (LLMs) proliferates across the internet, autonomous systems and AI training pipelines face an escalating structural risk: **Model Collapse (Autotoxicity)**—the progressive degradation of model performance caused by recursive training on machine-generated outputs.

Existing cryptographic signatures and content provenance standards (such as PKI or C2PA) verify **the container**—confirming *who* signed a file or *when* a timestamp was issued. However, they do not verify **the generation process**—whether the underlying data was produced through genuine physical measurement and iterative cognitive labor, or synthesized instantaneously without real-world grounding.

**Trust-Hash** is an open, low-layer verification protocol designed to restore physical and thermodynamic grounding to digital information. Rather than relying on heuristic "AI detectors" (an unwinnable arms race of AI vs. AI), Trust-Hash enforces a thermodynamic boundary: **it requires cryptographic and physical proof that irreversible time, energy, and iterative effort were expended to generate the data.**

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### 2. Core Philosophy: The Nutritional Label for Data
Trust-Hash operates as a **data refinery and labeling standard**, not a centralized arbiter of semantic truth.

1. **Thermodynamic Cost Asymmetry:** Generating authentic real-world telemetry or conducting physical R&D incurs unavoidable energy, time, and entropy costs. Trust-Hash binds digital records to these physical footprints so that the cost of forging provenance exceeds the cost of genuine generation.
2. **Distillation over Accumulation:** Rather than indexing all information, the protocol filters out ungrounded synthetic noise ("sludge") and distills raw inputs into **V-Fuel (Verified Fuel)**—high-purity, provenance-verified datasets safe for AI training, actuarial modeling, and scientific replication.
3. **Recursive Data Lineage:** Every verified artifact records its cryptographic ancestry via a Merkle-DAG structure, enabling downstream systems to trace whether an output was derived from verified primary sources or contaminated inputs.

#### 3. System Architecture: Triple-Filter Audit (TFA)
Raw inputs from R&D repositories, edge sensors, or research pipelines pass through the **Triple-Filter Audit (TFA)** before receiving a Trust-Hash Certificate.

##### Core Structural Equation
The protocol evaluates data integrity as a multiplicative gate across three orthogonal dimensions. If any single dimension fails verification, the resulting trust score collapses to zero:

$$T_{\text{hash}} = \Phi(NNS) \times \Delta I(PT) \times \mathcal{C}(W, IR, RF) \times \mathbb{I}(\text{Cost}_{\text{forge}} > \text{Cost}_{\text{real}})$$

* **$\Phi(NNS) \in [0, 1]$ (Statistical Naturalness):** Evaluates deviations from natural sensor entropy (e.g., Benford distributions and hardware noise floors) to detect over-smoothed synthetic generation.
* **$\Delta I(PT) \ge 0$ (Causal Topological Information):** Measures whether iterative trial-and-error logs expand the frontier of known parameter spaces, filtering out automated spam or meaningless error loops.
* **$\mathcal{C}(W, IR, RF) \in [0, 1]$ (Physical Cross-Correlation):** Verifies physical existence by cross-correlating compute power draw ($W$), thermal dissipation latency ($IR$), and ambient electromagnetic noise ($RF$) bound via Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs).
* **$\mathbb{I}(\dots) \in \{0, 1\}$ (Thermodynamic Gate):** Enforces that verification is only valid within domains where physical forgery cost strictly exceeds genuine execution cost.

*(Note: Specific cutoff thresholds, dynamic weighting matrices, and adversarial detection parameters are maintained within [Foundry Core / Proprietary Calibration] to prevent reverse-engineering by malicious actors.)*

#### 4. Tiered Verification & Privacy
To balance rigorous cryptographic assurance with practical adoption costs, Trust-Hash defines three verification tiers:

* **Tier 1 (Anchor / Hardware-Bound):** Direct ingestion from TEE-signed physical sensors, laboratory instruments, or verified institutional nodes with full TFA and Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) attestation.
* **Tier 2 (Verified / Process-Bound):** Audited R&D workflows, reproducible compute pipelines, and peer-validated datasets with verified causal lineage.
* **Tier 3 (Entry / Timestamp-Bound):** Lightweight cryptographic hashing and origin tagging for rapid onboarding, upgradable to higher tiers upon subsequent audit.

Through **Satori ZKP**, organizations can prove that proprietary datasets or internal R&D failure logs passed TFA verification without exposing underlying intellectual property or raw sensitive payloads.

#### 5. Scope, Non-Goals, and Falsifiability Conditions
To remain a neutral, auditable engineering standard, Trust-Hash explicitly defines its operational boundaries:

##### What Trust-Hash Does
* Proves that data originated from a physical process or genuine iterative effort.
* Enables privacy-preserving monetization and risk assessment of R&D failure logs and operational telemetry.
* Provides a deterministic filter for AI training pipelines to prevent Model Collapse.

##### Non-Goals (What Trust-Hash Does Not Do)
* **No Semantic or Moral Adjudication:** Trust-Hash certifies *origin and process*, not ideological correctness, moral value, or political truth. It is a nutritional label, not a censorship authority.
* **No General Social Media Moderation:** The protocol targets high-assurance domains (AI training corpora, scientific R&D, actuarial risk, and IP provenance), not general consumer chatter.
* **No Immunity Against High-Cost Physical Staging:** If an actor expends full real-world energy, time, and physical resources to stage a false experiment, the protocol records the physical execution accurately; deterrence relies on economic cost asymmetry.

##### Falsifiability & Failure Conditions
The theoretical premise of Trust-Hash is invalidated if any of the following conditions are empirically observed:
1. **Inversion of Thermodynamic Cost:** Analog hardware spoofing becomes cheaper and faster than executing genuine physical processes ($\text{Cost}_{\text{forge}} < \text{Cost}_{\text{real}}$).
2. **Unbounded Synthetic Self-Improvement:** AI models demonstrate sustained, open-ended capability scaling using 100% synthetic data without external real-world entropy or Model Collapse.
3. **Verification Cost Asphyxiation:** The computational and hardware overhead of proof generation permanently exceeds the economic value derived from verified data.
4. **Centralized Capture:** Cryptographic root keys or verification nodes are coerced or captured by a single state or corporate monopoly, breaking protocol neutrality.
